Eligibility Criteria

Who can apply?

  • Age Limit: 21 to 36 years (as of 09.08.2026).
  • Education: Min 50% in 10+2. Must have a Graduation Degree or be in the final year of Graduation.
  • Income: Total family income ≤ Rs. 8.00 lakh/annum.
  • Reservation: 30% of seats are strictly reserved for Female candidates.
Financial Support

Benefits & Stipend

Coaching is provided via Scholarship based on the Merit List.

  • Monthly Stipend: Rs. 4,000/month disbursed via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) to an Aadhaar-seeded bank account.
  • Mains Incentive: Rs. 15,000 provided to candidates clearing the mains stage (Group 1/2) for interview preparation.
Strict Guidelines

Rules & Regulations

  • Attendance: Physical mode attendance is mandatory.
  • Absence: Missing >4 days without prior permission will result in immediate scholarship cancellation.
  • Midway Dropouts: Leaving the program midway without approval will result in the recovery of incurred expenditure.
Application Checklist

Required Documents

All documents must be in .jpeg or .pdf format and less than 500 kb (unless specified). Only online applications accepted.

  • Passport-size photo (.jpeg, <100kb)
  • Signature (.jpeg, <100kb)
  • Proof of Age (10th Certificate)
  • Intermediate (12th) Marksheet
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Graduation Degree & Marksheet
  • * Caste Certificate (Mandatory)
  • * Latest Income Certificate from Revenue Officer (Tehsildar rank or above)
  • Personal Email ID & Mobile Number
