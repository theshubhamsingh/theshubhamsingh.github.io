Admissions Open for UPSC/State-PSCs (Batch 2025-26 - 3rd Round)

Only online applications will be accepted. Candidates will be selected based on the DACE Entrance Examination 2025-26.

Category A

Eligibility Criteria

  • Target Group: SC and OBC Students.
  • Age Limit: 21 to 36 years (as of 09.08.2026).
  • Education: Min 50% in 10+2. Graduation Degree or studying in final year.
  • Income: Total family income ≤ Rs. 8.00 lakh/annum.
  • Reservation: 30% of seats are reserved for Female candidates.
Financial Support

Benefits & Stipend

Scholarship based on Merit list (Till March 2027).

  • Monthly Stipend: Rs. 4,000/month via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
  • Mains Incentive: Rs. 15,000 provided to candidates clearing the mains stage to prepare for interviews.
Mandatory Rules

Guidelines

  • Attendance: Physical mode attendance is mandatory.
  • Absence: Absence of >4 days without prior permission will result in scholarship cancellation.
  • Payment: Valid Aadhaar number mandatory for DBT disbursements to Aadhaar Seeded bank accounts.
Application Checklist

Required Documents

All documents must be in .jpeg or .pdf format and less than 500 kb. Fields marked with * are mandatory.

  • * Passport-size photograph (< 100 kb)
  • * Signature (< 100 kb)
  • * Proof of Age (10th Certificate)
  • * Intermediate (12th) Marksheet
  • * Aadhaar Card
  • * Graduation Degree & Marksheet
  • * Caste Certificate (SC/OBC)
  • * Latest Income Certificate (Tehsildar rank or above)
  • * Personal Email ID & Mobile Number
